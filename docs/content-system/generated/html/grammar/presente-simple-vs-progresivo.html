Learn the difference between the present simple (presente simple) and the present progressive (estar + gerundio). Use short sentences to talk about habits and actions happening now.

Overview

Present simplePresent progressive
Habits and routinesActions in progress

Todos los días — Every dayAhora — Now

Presente simple: conjugate the main verb. Presente progresivo: use estar + gerundio (estoy, estás, está, estamos, estáis, están + -ando / -iendo).

Present simple — Habits and routines

Use the present simple to talk about routines, habits and facts.

Yo estudio. — I study.

Tú comes. — You eat.

Ella vive aquí. — She lives here.

Nosotros trabajamos. — We work.

Present progressive — Actions happening now

Use estar + gerundio to describe an action happening now.

Estoy estudiando. — I am studying.

Estás comiendo. — You are eating.

Ella está leyendo. — She is reading.

Estamos trabajando. — We are working.

Examples: hablar → hablando, comer → comiendo, vivir → viviendo. Some gerunds are irregular, such as leer → leyendo.

Compare — Compara

Routine / Now

Estudio español todos los días. — I study Spanish every day.

Estoy estudiando español ahora. — I am studying Spanish now.

Routine / Now

Ana trabaja en una tienda. — Ana works in a store.

Ana está trabajando ahora. — Ana is working now.

Routine / Now

Comemos en casa los domingos. — We eat at home on Sundays.

Estamos comiendo en casa. — We are eating at home.

Routine / Now

Él lee por la noche. — He reads at night.

Él está leyendo ahora. — He is reading now.

Routine / Now

Ellos juegan los sábados. — They play on Saturdays.

Ellos están jugando. — They are playing.
