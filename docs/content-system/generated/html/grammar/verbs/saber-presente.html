Use saber to talk about information and skills. In the present tense, the yo form is sé.

Overview

Saber means to know. Use it with information or an infinitive to describe a skill.

Sé la respuesta. — I know the answer.

Sé nadar. — I know how to swim.

Conjugation — Present indicative

Only yo sé is irregular. The other forms use the regular present endings.

Uses

Information — Información

Use saber to talk about facts or information.

Sé la respuesta. — I know the answer.

Ella sabe mi nombre. — She knows my name.

¿Sabes la dirección? — Do you know the address?

Skills — Habilidades

Use saber + infinitive to say that someone knows how to do something.

Sé nadar. — I know how to swim.

Tú sabes cocinar. — You know how to cook.

Ellos saben bailar. — They know how to dance.

Facts with que — Saber que

Use saber que before a fact.

Sé que Ana está aquí. — I know that Ana is here.

Sabemos que hoy es lunes. — We know that today is Monday.

Oraciones

Afirmativas

Yo sé la respuesta. — I know the answer.

Tú sabes nadar. — You know how to swim.

Ella sabe mi nombre. — She knows my name.

Negativas

No sé la respuesta. — I do not know the answer.

Él no sabe cocinar. — He does not know how to cook.

No sabemos la dirección. — We do not know the address.

Preguntas

¿Sabes nadar? — Do you know how to swim?

¿Sabe usted la respuesta? — Do you know the answer?

¿Ellas saben cantar? — Do they know how to sing?
