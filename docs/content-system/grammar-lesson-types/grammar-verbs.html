Quick question

How would you say: I am Steve Rogers.

When do we use ser?

Ser is one of the main ways to use to be in Spanish.
  • Yo soy Steve Rogers. → I am Steve Rogers.
In this example, soy expresses identity. But we can use ser to express other things, like:

Names

Él es Steve Rogers. He is Steve Rogers.

Nationality

Ella es mexicana. She is Mexican.

Profession

Él es médico. He is a doctor.
Ser is an irregular verb, so you might need to memorize a little.

Present tense

Subject Form Spanish English
yo soy Yo soy Steve. I am Steve.
tú eres Tú eres Tony. You are Tony.
él / ella / usted es Ella es Natalia. She is Natalia.
nosotros / nosotras somos Nosotros somos amigos. We are friends.
ellos / ellas / ustedes son Ellos son estudiantes. They are students.

Names

We use ser to talk about names.

Affirmative

Él es Steve Rogers. He is Steve Rogers. Ella es Wanda Maximoff. She is Wanda Maximoff.

Negative

Él no es Loki. He is not Loki. Ella no es Natasha Romanoff. She is not Natasha Romanoff.

Questions

¿Él es Peter Parker? Is he Peter Parker? ¿Ella es Wanda Maximoff? Is she Wanda Maximoff?
