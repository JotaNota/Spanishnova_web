Why support SpanishNova?

SpanishNova was created to help learners practice Spanish through clear explanations, useful resources and content connected to real communication.

Your support helps keep materials free and accessible for students and teachers. It also helps create more readings, conversations, vocabulary lessons, exercises and low-cost courses.

What does your contribution support?

  • More readings, conversations and podcast-style lessons.
  • More vocabulary and grammar resources.
  • More exercises for learners at different levels.
  • Site maintenance and future improvements.
  • Affordable courses with videos and structured lessons.
