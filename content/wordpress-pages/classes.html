How to get started

  1. Tell us your level. If you are unsure, describe what you can do in Spanish.
  2. Share your goals. Tell us whether you want to work on conversation, grammar, vocabulary or another area.
  3. Ask about availability. Contact SpanishNova to discuss class options.

What can you practice?

  • Speaking and everyday conversations.
  • Listening and comprehension.
  • Vocabulary for situations you want to discuss.
  • Grammar applied to communication.
