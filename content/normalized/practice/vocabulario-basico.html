Examples

Trabajar

Trabajar en la casa — To work at home

Trabajar en la oficina — To work in an office

Hablar

Hablar con amigos — To speak with friends

Vivir

Vivir en una ciudad — To live in a city

Vivir en una casa — To live in a house

Vivir en un apartamento — To live in an apartment

Ir

Ir a la escuela — To go to school

Ir al trabajo — To go to work

Ir al cine — To go to the movies

Beber

Beber agua — To drink water

Beber jugo — To drink juice

Beber té — To drink tea

Beber café — To drink coffee

Comer

Comer pizza — To eat pizza

Comer arroz — To eat rice

Comer fruta — To eat fruit

Desayunar

Desayunar pan, café y jugo — To have breakfast with bread, coffee, and juice

Almorzar

Almorzar ensalada — To have lunch with salad

Almorzar sándwich y sopa — To have lunch with a sandwich and soup

Cenar

Cenar pasta — To have dinner with pasta

Cenar carne y ensalada — To have dinner with meat and salad

Caminar

Caminar al trabajo — To walk to work

Caminar al parque — To walk to the park

Caminar por la ciudad — To walk around the city

Hacer

Hacer la comida — To make food

Hacer la tarea — To do homework

Hacer ejercicio — To do exercise

Hacer un proyecto — To do a project

Tener

Tener una casa — To have a house

Tener un carro — To have a car

Tener amigos — To have friends

Tener tiempo — To have time

Pedir

Pedir comida — To order food

Pedir en un restaurante — To ask for something in a restaurant

Pedir ayuda — To ask for help
