Possessive adjectives show who owns something. In Spanish, mi, tu, su, and nuestro go before the noun they describe.

Overview

Possessive adjectives agree with the thing possessed. Their form depends on the noun that follows, not on the owner's gender.

Singular and plural

Mi libro está aquí. — My book is here.

Mis libros están aquí. — My books are here.

Mi, tu, and su change between singular and plural according to the noun: mi libro / mis libros; tu amigo / tus amigos; su pregunta / sus preguntas.

Nuestro changes for masculine, feminine, singular, and plural nouns: nuestro libro, nuestra casa, nuestros libros, nuestras casas.

Tu (without an accent) is possessive; tú (with an accent) is the subject pronoun.

Su / sus can refer to different owners. Context helps identify whose noun it is.

Uses

Possessive adjectives before the noun

Place mi, tu, su, and nuestro before the noun. The singular and plural forms describe how many things are possessed.

Tu teléfono está en la mesa. — Your phone is on the table.

Sus amigos viven en Chile. — His, her, your formal, or their friends live in Chile.

Our: gender and number

Nuestro agrees with the noun in both gender and number.

Nuestra clase empieza ahora. — Our class starts now.

Nuestros profesores trabajan aquí. — Our teachers work here.

Examples in context

Afirmativas

Mi libro está aquí. — My book is here.

Mis libros están aquí. — My books are here.

Tu amigo es simpático. — Your friend is nice.

Tus amigos son simpáticos. — Your friends are nice.

Su pregunta es buena. — His, her, your formal, or their question is good.

Sus preguntas son buenas. — His, her, your formal, or their questions are good.

Nuestra casa es grande. — Our house is big.

Nuestras casas son grandes. — Our houses are big.

Negativas

Mi teléfono no funciona. — My phone does not work.

Mis llaves no están aquí. — My keys are not here.

Tu mochila no es azul. — Your backpack is not blue.

Tus respuestas no son correctas. — Your answers are not correct.

Su oficina no está abierta. — His, her, your formal, or their office is not open.

Sus hijos no están en casa. — His, her, your formal, or their children are not at home.

Nuestro profesor no está aquí. — Our teacher is not here.

Nuestros libros no están en la mesa. — Our books are not on the table.

Preguntas

¿Es tu mochila? — Is it your backpack?

¿Son tus amigos? — Are they your friends?

¿Su pregunta es importante? — Is his, her, your formal, or their question important?

¿Sus preguntas son buenas? — Are his, her, your formal, or their questions good?

¿Nuestra clase empieza ahora? — Does our class start now?

¿Nuestros libros están aquí? — Are our books here?

¿Dónde están mis llaves? — Where are my keys?

¿Dónde está su oficina? — Where is his, her, your formal, or their office?
