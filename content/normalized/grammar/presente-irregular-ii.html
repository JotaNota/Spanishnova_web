Quick question

How would you say:

I make coffee.

Overview

Many common Spanish verbs are irregular in the present indicative. Some change the stem, some change only the yo form, and some have unique forms.

In Spanish, many common verbs are not completely regular in the present tense.

Stem changes

querer → quiero, quieres, quiere — to want
poder → puedo, puedes, puede — can / to be able to

Irregular yo form

hacer → yo hago — to do / to make
poner → yo pongo — to put

Other irregular verbs

ser → soy, eres, es — to be
ir → voy, vas, va — to go

Structure

Sujeto + verbo irregular en presente + complemento

  • Yo hago café por la mañana. → I make coffee in the morning.

Oraciones

Affirmative

Mi hermana quiere estudiar medicina. — My sister wants to study medicine.
Los niños duermen temprano durante la semana. — The children sleep early during the week.
Yo hago café por la mañana. — I make coffee in the morning.
Nosotros vamos al centro los sábados. — We go downtown on Saturdays.
La profesora trae los libros a clase. — The teacher brings the books to class.

Negative

No sé la dirección exacta. — I do not know the exact address.
Mis amigos no pueden venir hoy. — My friends cannot come today.
Carlos no dice la verdad. — Carlos does not tell the truth.
Nosotros no tenemos clase mañana. — We do not have class tomorrow.
Ella no está en la oficina. — She is not in the office.

Questions

¿Prefieres café o té? — Do you prefer coffee or tea?
¿A qué hora sale el tren? — What time does the train leave?
¿Ustedes vienen a la reunión? — Are you all coming to the meeting?
¿Quién pone la música? — Who puts on the music?
¿Dónde están las llaves? — Where are the keys?
