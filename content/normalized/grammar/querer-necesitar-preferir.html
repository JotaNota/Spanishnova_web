Quick question

How would you say:

I want chocolate.

Use querer, necesitar, and preferir to express what someone wants, needs, or prefers.

Overview

These verbs can be used with a noun or with an infinitive verb.

  • Yo quiero chocolate. — I want chocolate.
  • Yo quiero comer. — I want to eat.
  • Yo necesito una respuesta. — I need an answer.
  • Yo prefiero estudiar en silencio. — I prefer to study in silence.

Querer

Querer means to want.

Use querer to talk about things someone wants or actions someone wants to do.

Structure

Structure Example Translation
querer + noun Yo quiero un café. I want a coffee.
querer + infinitive Yo quiero comer. I want to eat.
no + querer + infinitive Ella no quiere trabajar. She does not want to work.

Conjugation of “querer” in the present tense

Querer is irregular. Some forms change from e to ie.

Subject pronoun Querer Example Translation
Yo quiero Yo quiero chocolate. I want chocolate.
Tú quieres Tú quieres aprender español. You want to learn Spanish.
Él / Ella / Usted quiere Ella quiere viajar. She wants to travel.
Nosotros / Nosotras queremos Nosotros queremos comer pizza. We want to eat pizza.
Ellos / Ellas quieren Ellos quieren estudiar medicina. They want to study medicine.
Ustedes quieren Ustedes quieren estudiar ahora. You all want to study now.

Negative

Questions

Necesitar and preferir

Necesitar means to need.

Preferir means to prefer.

Use both verbs with a noun or with an infinitive verb.

Conjugation of “necesitar” and “preferir”

Necesitar is regular. Preferir is irregular. Some forms change from e to ie.

Subject pronoun Necesitar Preferir
Yo necesito prefiero
Tú necesitas prefieres
Él / Ella / Usted necesita prefiere
Nosotros / Nosotras necesitamos preferimos
Ellos / Ellas / Ustedes necesitan prefieren

Examples with “necesitar” and “preferir”

Affirmative

Yo necesito estudiar para el examen. — I need to study for the exam.
Tú prefieres el chocolate. — You prefer chocolate.

Negative

Yo no necesito comer ahora. — I do not need to eat now.
Nosotros no preferimos ese restaurante. — We do not prefer that restaurant.

Questions

¿Qué necesitas hoy? — What do you need today?
¿Prefieres té o café? — Do you prefer tea or coffee?

Wrap-up

Use these verbs to express wants, needs, and preferences.

Verb Use Example Translation

Necesitar Need Yo necesito una respuesta. I need an answer.
