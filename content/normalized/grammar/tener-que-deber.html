Quick question

How would you say:

I have to work.

Overview

Use tener que and deber to express obligations or things someone needs to do:

Tener que + infinitive means to have to.

→ Use it to express obligation.

  • Yo tengo que trabajar. → I have to work.

Deber + infinitive means should or must.

→ Use it to express advice, duty, or obligation.

  • Yo debo estudiar más. → I should study more.

Structure

Subject + tener + que + infinitive verb

  • Yo tengo que estudiar. → I have to study.
  • Ella tiene que cocinar. → She has to cook.
  • Nosotros tenemos que trabajar. → We have to work.

Subject + deber + infinitive verb

  • Yo debo estudiar. → I should study.
  • Ella debe descansar. → She should rest.
  • Nosotros debemos practicar. → We should practice.

In both structures, the second verb stays in the infinitive form.

Common uses

There are different uses of tener que and deber, like:

  • Plans or tasks you must do
  • Obligations
  • Responsibilities
  • Advice

Let’s see examples of each one.

Affirmative

Yo tengo que estudiar hoy. — I have to study today.
Ella debe descansar más. — She must rest more.
Nosotros tenemos que comprar comida. — We have to buy food.
Tú debes practicar español. — You must practice Spanish.

Negative

Yo no tengo que trabajar mañana. — I don’t have to work tomorrow.
Ella no debe dormir tarde. — She must not sleep late.
Nosotros no tenemos que salir ahora. — We don’t have to leave now.
Tú no debes comer azúcar. — You must not eat sugar.

Questions

¿Tú tienes que estudiar hoy? — Do you have to study today?
¿Ella debe descansar más? — Should she rest more?
¿Nosotros tenemos que terminar esto ahora? — Do we have to finish this now?
¿Ellos deben practicar más? — Should they practice more?

Wrap up

Use these structures for things you have to do or must do:

  • Tener que + infinitive
  • Deber + infinitive
Structure Example Translation
Tener que Yo tengo que estudiar. I have to study.
Tener que Ella tiene que trabajar mañana. She has to work tomorrow.
Deber Tú debes practicar español. You must practice Spanish.
Deber Nosotros debemos llegar temprano. We must arrive early.
