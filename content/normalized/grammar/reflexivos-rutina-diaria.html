Quick question

How would you say:

I bathe myself.

Overview

Reflexive verbs are used when the action goes back to the same person who does the action.

Compare these examples:

An action on something else

Yo baño al perro. — I bathe the dog.

An action on yourself

Yo me baño. — I bathe myself.

In Spanish, we normally use a reflexive pronoun before the conjugated verb.

Structure

Subject + reflexive pronoun + conjugated verb

Reflexive pronouns

Yo me baño. — I bathe myself.
Tú te bañas. — You bathe yourself.
Ella se baña. — She bathes herself.

Conjugation

Pronombre Reflexivo Ejemplo
Yo me me baño
Tú te te bañas
Él / Ella / Usted se se baña
Nosotros/as nos nos bañamos
Ustedes se se bañan
Ellos/as se se bañan

Common routine verbs

Español English
ducharse to take a shower
bañarse to take a bath / to bathe
lavarse las manos to wash one's hands
lavarse la cara to wash one's face
peinarse to comb one's hair
afeitarse to shave
maquillarse to put on makeup
cepillarse los dientes to brush one's teeth
secarse to dry oneself

Oraciones

Affirmative

Yo me ducho por la mañana. — I take a shower in the morning.
Tú te peinas antes de salir. — You comb your hair before going out.
Ella se maquilla todos los días. — She puts on makeup every day.
Nosotros nos lavamos las manos antes de comer. — We wash our hands before eating.
Ellos se cepillan los dientes por la noche. — They brush their teeth at night.
Yo me seco con la toalla. — I dry myself with the towel.

Negative

Tú no te afeitas por la mañana. — You do not shave in the morning.
Ella no se lava el cabello todos los días. — She does not wash her hair every day.
Ellos no se bañan en la mañana. — They do not take a bath in the morning.
Yo no me maquillo para trabajar. — I do not put on makeup for work.
Nosotros no nos secamos rápido. — We do not dry ourselves quickly.

Questions

¿Usted se ducha por la mañana? — Do you take a shower in the morning?
¿Se peinan ustedes antes de salir? — Do you all comb your hair before going out?
¿Por qué no te peinas? — Why don't you comb your hair?
¿A qué hora te cepillas los dientes? — What time do you brush your teeth?
¿Te secas el pelo con la toalla? — Do you dry your hair with the towel?

Reflexive verbs with auxiliary verbs

When a reflexive verb follows a conjugated verb such as querer, necesitar or ir a, the pronoun can go before the conjugated verb or attach to the infinitive. With estar + gerund, it can also attach to the gerund.

Action in progress

Yo me estoy duchando. — I am taking a shower.
Yo estoy duchándome. — I am taking a shower.

Want, need or intention

Yo me quiero duchar. — I want to take a shower.
Yo quiero ducharme. — I want to take a shower.
Yo necesito ducharme. — I need to take a shower.
Yo me voy a duchar. — I am going to take a shower.
Yo voy a ducharme. — I am going to take a shower.

Both pronoun positions are valid in the constructions shown.

Oraciones con auxiliar

Affirmative

Yo me estoy duchando. — I am taking a shower.
Yo estoy duchándome. — I am taking a shower.
Tú te estás peinando. — You are combing your hair.
Tú estás peinándote. — You are combing your hair.
Ella se va a maquillar. — She is going to put on makeup.
Ella va a maquillarse. — She is going to put on makeup.
Nosotros nos estamos secando. — We are drying ourselves.
Nosotros estamos secándonos. — We are drying ourselves.

Negative

Yo no me estoy afeitando. — I am not shaving.
Yo no estoy afeitándome. — I am not shaving.
Ellos no se están bañando. — They are not taking a bath.
Ellos no están bañándose. — They are not taking a bath.
Ella no se está secando el pelo. — She is not drying her hair.
Ella no está secándose el pelo. — She is not drying her hair.

Questions

¿Te estás duchando? — Are you taking a shower?
¿Estás duchándote? — Are you taking a shower?
¿Se están peinando ustedes? — Are you all combing your hair?
¿Están peinándose ustedes? — Are you all combing your hair?
¿Te estás secando el pelo? — Are you drying your hair?
¿Estás secándote el pelo? — Are you drying your hair?
